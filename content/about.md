I'm dualface. I've been deeply involved in open source for more than 20 years.

Early on, I wrote FleaPHP and QeePHP, among the first open-source PHP frameworks in China. Later I created quick-cocos2d-x, a game engine. When it was acquired, about a third of mobile game teams in China were using it.

Today I'm a solo developer. My main focus is making an indie game, and I build developer tools along the way.

## What I write about

Here I share my experience and lessons from working with a team of AI agents, building developer tools, and making an indie game, along with original tutorials I write.

Topics include:

- How to work with AI agents
- The developer tools I build
- The indie game I'm making

## Projects

- [Kander](https://github.com/dualface/kander): a kanban board for coordinating multiple AI agents
- [QuickTUI](https://quicktui.ai): control the coding agents on your computer from your phone

## How I write these posts

I write the original posts in Chinese, my native language. The English versions are translated with AI assistance.
